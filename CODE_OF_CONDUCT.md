# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for contributors and users, regardless of experience or background.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, patient, and constructive.
- Respect different viewpoints and levels of experience.
- Give actionable technical feedback.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not individuals.
- Protect other people's privacy.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, doxxing, and deliberately disruptive behavior are not tolerated.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer using an appropriate private contact channel. Do not include sensitive personal information in public issues. The maintainer will review reports confidentially where practicable and may remove content, warn participants, or restrict participation.

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces.
